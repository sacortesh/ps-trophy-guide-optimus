Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 2024
Effective Date: December 2024

This Privacy Policy describes how PS Trophy Guide Optimus ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use our Chrome extension.

1. Information We Collect

Data Extracted from TrueTrophies

Our extension extracts the following trophy-related data from TrueTrophies pages:

  • Trophy titles and descriptions
  • Trophy types (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum)
  • Rarity percentages
  • Trophy tags and categories
  • Completion status
  • Guide URLs

Technical Information

The extension may collect minimal technical information necessary for operation:

  • Extension usage statistics (locally stored)
  • Error logs for debugging purposes
  • Browser compatibility information

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • Data Extraction: To extract trophy data from TrueTrophies pages
  • CSV Export: To format and export trophy data in CSV format
  • Local Storage: To temporarily store extracted data for export
  • Extension Functionality: To provide the core features of the extension

3. Data Storage and Processing

Important: All data processing happens locally in your browser. We do not transmit, store, or access your data on external servers.

Local Storage

Extracted trophy data is stored locally in your browser's storage and is:

  • Only accessible to the extension
  • Automatically cleared when you clear browser data
  • Not transmitted to any external servers
  • Completely under your control

No External Data Transmission

We do not:

  • Send your data to external servers
  • Share your data with third parties
  • Use your data for advertising or marketing
  • Track your browsing activity outside of TrueTrophies

4. Permissions and Access

Our extension requests the following permissions:

  • activeTab: To access the current TrueTrophies page for data extraction
  • downloads: To save CSV files to your device
  • storage: To temporarily store extracted data locally
  • host_permissions: Limited to TrueTrophies.com domain only

5. Third-Party Services

Our extension integrates with:

  • TrueTrophies.com: We extract data from this site only when you visit it
  • Chrome Web Store: For extension distribution and updates

We are not affiliated with TrueTrophies or Sony Interactive Entertainment.

6. Data Security

We implement the following security measures:

  • All data processing occurs locally in your browser
  • No external data transmission or storage
  • Minimal permission requests limited to necessary functionality
  • Regular security updates and maintenance

7. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: All data is stored locally and accessible through your browser
  • Deletion: Clear browser data or uninstall the extension to remove all data
  • Control: You control when and what data is extracted
  • Export: You can export your data in CSV format at any time

8. Children's Privacy

Our extension is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy on this page
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date
  • Notifying users through the extension update process

10. Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@example.com
  • GitHub: Project Repository
  • Chrome Web Store: Extension listing page

👋 About

Hi there! I'm Sergio, and I'm passionate about making trophy hunting more efficient and enjoyable for everyone.

As a casual trophy hunter myself, I've spent countless hours manually copying trophy data from TrueTrophies into spreadsheets. I love analyzing data, optimizing workflows, and finding better ways to track my gaming progress. That's exactly why I created PS Trophy Extractor.

This Chrome extension was born from my own frustration with repetitive manual work. Instead of spending hours copying trophy lists, completion percentages, and rarity data by hand, I wanted a tool that could do it all with a single click and export everything to CSV format.

While I may not have as much time for trophy hunting these days, I believe in giving back to the gaming community. This tool is my way of helping fellow trophy hunters focus on what matters most – enjoying the games and celebrating those hard-earned achievements.

PS Trophy Extractor is completely free and open-source. If you find it useful and want to support its development, consider buying me a coffee. Your support helps keep this tool maintained and free for everyone.

📥 Installation Guide

Navigate to the extension store in your Chrome browser

2

Install the extension

Click "Add to Chrome" and follow the prompts

3

Pin the extension

Click the puzzle piece icon in Chrome toolbar and pin the extension for easy access

4

Navigate to a TrueTrophies game trophy page and click the extension icon to extract trophy data

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

The extension extracts comprehensive trophy data including:

  • Trophy title and description
  • Trophy type (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum)
  • Sony rarity percentage
  • TrueTrophies rarity data
  • Trophy tags (Missable, Collectable, etc.)

Additionally, links to video guides and a custom score for each trophy are generated.

Yes! All data processing happens locally in your browser. The extension:

  • Does not send any data to external servers
  • Only accesses TrueTrophies pages you visit
  • Stores data locally in your browser
  • Respects your privacy completely

The extension works at the moment only on TrueTrophies game trophy pages with URLs like:

https://www.truetrophies.com/game/[game-name]/trophies

It automatically detects when you're on a supported page and enables the extraction features.

If extraction fails, try these troubleshooting steps:

  • Refresh the TrueTrophies page and try again
  • Make sure you're on a game's trophy page (not the main game page)
  • Check that all trophies have loaded on the page
  • Use the "Test Connection" button in the extension popup
  • Check the browser console for detailed error messages
